#  Pizza Sales & Delivery Performance Dashboard (2024)

##  Project Overview
An interactive multi-page Excel dashboard analyzing pizza sales, delivery performance, customer behavior, and payment insights throughout 2024.

##  Project Objectives
- Analyze sales performance.
- Evaluate delivery efficiency.
- Understand customer preferences.
- Identify peak ordering hours.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

##  Dashboard Insights

### 1. Sales Overview
- Total Orders: 1,004
- Top Pizza Chain: Domino's (212 orders)
- Most Popular Size: Medium (429 orders)
- Peak Sales Months: August & September
- Top Cities: Atlanta & Milwaukee

### 2. Delivery Performance
- Average Delivery Time: 29.5 minutes
- Average Delivery Distance: 4.95 km
- On-Time Deliveries: 794
- Delayed Deliveries: 210
- Peak Hours: 6 PM – 8 PM

### 3. Customer & Payment Insights
- Digital Payments: More than 75%
- Cash Payments: 202 orders
- Popular Categories: Non-Veg, Veg, Cheese Burst

##  Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Pivot Tables
- Calculated Fields
- Interactive Slicers
- Data Visualization

##  Key Business Insights
- Domino's recorded the highest number of orders.
- Medium-sized pizzas were the most popular.
- Evening hours experienced the highest demand.
- Digital payments dominated customer transactions.
